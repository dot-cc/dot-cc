Why the market is moving

The Saudi CX market
could quadruple by 2032.

Vision 2030 turned customer experience into a national score. Read the numbers as direction, not precision: research firms disagree on size.

Sources: MarketsandMarkets (CX management), IMARC (cloud contact centre).

Government is measured

Adaa, the national performance centre, runs a mystery shopper programme. Agencies need measurement partners.

Saudization

Remote customer service jobs are reserved for Saudi nationals; hotel call centre agents 100% from 22 April 2026. Favors local operators, raises hiring cost.

Data stays home

The Personal Data Protection Law and in-Kingdom hosting are now buying criteria. Global clouds have local regions too.

AI in Arabic

Buyers ask for AI agents that understand dialects, with proof of results. Lucidya raised $30M on exactly this.
