Two disciplines, one team

The dot. method

Five steps, always in this order. The order is the method.

Listen

We start in your room, with your people. Your goals, your story, your market, and what has already been tried.

Find

We build the real numbers ourselves. Cost, margin, win rate, and where your best clients truly came from.

Fix

What you sell, what you charge, how you sell it, and how it gets delivered. In that order.

Express

Now the brand has something true to stand on. Positioning, message, identity and campaign, built on the fix.

Hand over

Your team runs it. We train the owners, watch them run it alone, then step back to advise.

Most rebrands that fail skipped steps two and three. We never do.
