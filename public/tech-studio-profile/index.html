Company profile

AI and automation, business systems, data, rescue and compliance, and technology leadership, for companies in Saudi Arabia.

Technology that holds when the deadline is real.

dot. tech studio builds and runs the systems a company depends on: the quotation, the ledger, the integration, the report the owner reads every morning. We are the team you call when the vendor has failed and the date will not move.

Our work comes from more than 19 years inside Saudi insurers, payment companies, factories and food technology, where the numbers have to reconcile and the regulator decides the calendar.

  • AI agents and assistants that take the routine off your team, with the budget and the run logs reported to you.
